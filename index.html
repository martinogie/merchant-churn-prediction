1. Predicting churn

A Random Forest scores each merchant monthly from point-in-time features: volume against its own three-month average, seasonally adjusted trend, active days, failed payments, payout delays and support tickets. SMOTE balances the training months only, and the alert threshold is tuned on a later month before testing on August and September.

Accuracy is 92.3%, but with 9.5% of merchants churning, accuracy says little. Precision at the alert threshold sets how much account-manager time is wasted.

2. Seasonal dip or product problem?

In July, merchants on the plugin integration moved to a new payout verification flow. Volume also swings with the seasons, so a before/after comparison mixes the two. Difference-in-differences with merchant and month fixed effects compares plugin merchants to everyone else over the same months.

The friction cut plugin merchants' volume by -8.3% (95% CI -9.1% to -7.5%). The groups moved together before July (pre-trend p = 0.16).

3. Does early outreach work?

Merchants flagged in September were randomly split. Half got an account-manager call within 24 hours; half got nothing extra.

Scoring in production

A Flask endpoint returns a churn probability, risk tier and recommended action for each merchant, with p95 latency under 50 ms in tests. A Dockerfile packages the API with a trained model.

All data is synthetic, generated with known effects so every method can be checked against the truth; the A/B outcomes are simulated from an assumed outreach effect. Built with AI-assisted coding (Claude); modeling choices, validation and review by Martins Ogiekpolor.
